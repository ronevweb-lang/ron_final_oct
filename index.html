About RON Mobility

Building India's Next Generation of Electric Commercial Mobility.

RON Mobility is an Indian electric commercial vehicle company committed to redefining commercial transportation through advanced, reliable and purpose-built electric vehicles.

Our vision is to establish a comprehensive electric commercial vehicle portfolio spanning LCVs, trucks, buses and coaches — supported by an integrated ecosystem of technology, manufacturing, sales and after-sales service.

Built for India. Developed for global markets. RON is creating the electric commercial vehicles that will shape the future of transportation.

Our Approach

More Than Electric. Built for Business.

Commercial mobility demands more than electrification. It demands performance, reliability, efficiency and uptime — every day, across every route and every load. RON Mobility approaches commercial EV development with a clear purpose: to combine advanced electric technology with intelligent engineering and real-world commercial practicality.

Founder's Message

Our Vision. Our Revolution. Our Responsibility.

RON stands for Revolution on EV.

For us, RON is more than a name. It represents a belief that India can build a complete, self-sustaining electric mobility ecosystem of its own—one that is designed for Indian businesses, Indian roads, and the future of transportation.

We started RON Mobility with a simple question:

Why should India depend on conventional mobility when we have the opportunity to build an electric future ourselves?

Our vision is to create a comprehensive range of all-electric commercial vehicles that can serve businesses across different segments—from affordable, practical work vehicles to premium, high-performance commercial vehicles.

We want electric mobility to be accessible to everyone, without compromising on what matters most.

Affordable. Premium. Long-range. Reliable. Practical. Easy to maintain. Easy to service.

These are not just product features for us. They are the principles on which we want to build RON.

We understand that a commercial vehicle is not simply a vehicle. For many customers, it is their business, their livelihood, and their everyday partner. Every hour on the road matters. Every kilometre matters. Every service visit matters. That is why our approach to electric mobility goes beyond developing a powertrain or putting a battery into a vehicle.

We want to build vehicles that people can trust, operate, maintain and depend on every day.

Our ambition is to develop RON into a complete electric commercial mobility company—with a growing portfolio of vehicles, strong technology, dependable after-sales support, accessible service, and an ecosystem capable of supporting our customers throughout the life of their vehicles.

We believe the future of mobility should be electric, but it must also be practical and sustainable for the people who use it.

As founders, we know that building this vision will take time. There will be challenges, failures, lessons and constant evolution. But our commitment remains unchanged: to keep building, keep learning, and keep moving forward.

We are not here simply to follow the EV revolution.

We want to build it.

From India, for India—and ultimately, for the world.

This is RON.
This is our Revolution On EV.
